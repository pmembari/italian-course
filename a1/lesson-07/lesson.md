## Goals and learning method

Read vacation offers and personal emails; talk about past holidays using **passato prossimo**, **avere/essere** and irregular participles; discuss weather and geography; describe past-time expressions; and complete the combined Lessons 6–7 review.

Use **see → say → sentence → dialogue → hidden recall**. Each source activity below gets an original model dialogue and a book-specific picture/reading task. The photographs in this GitHub course are licensed substitutes, **not copied publisher art**.

## Reading mission — original practice (not the book's text)

> **Una vacanza inventata** — La settimana scorsa Lara **è andata** al lago. Sabato **ha preso** una barca e **ha fatto** delle fotografie. Domenica **è rimasta** in albergo perché **ha piovuto**. Alla fine **è tornata** a casa e **ha letto** un libro. È stata una vacanza tranquillissima.

**Questions:** Dove è andata Lara? Che cosa ha fatto sabato? Perché è rimasta in albergo? Quali sono due participi irregolari?

<details><summary>Reveal answers</summary>

Al lago; ha preso una barca e ha fatto delle fotografie; perché ha piovuto; per esempio **preso**, **fatto**, **rimasta** (from *rimanere*) and **letto**. The dialogue also contains the regular participles **andata** and **tornata**.

</details>

## Shadowing and retrieval

- **Repeat aloud** the original companion exchange for each activity. First understand, then echo line by line, then speak without looking, then create your own exchange.
- **True audio shadowing** requires a lawful, accessible Italian recording. The textbook's audio/video is not republished here; do not claim listening tasks are complete from printed pages alone.
- **Spaced recall:** try again tomorrow, in three days and after seven days, retrieving whole sentences before checking the examples.
- **English comparisons** and grammar memory cues are in [Grammar](grammar.md); [Vocabulary](vocabulary.md) adds photo thumbnails beside concrete terms and emojis for abstract terms.
