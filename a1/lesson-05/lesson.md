# Lezione 5 — In albergo

[← A1 menu](../README.md) · **Overview** · [Grammar](grammar.md) · [Vocabulary](vocabulary.md) · [Exercise](exercise.md)

> **Book accommodation and describe rooms, amenities and dates.** Follow *Nuovo Espresso 1*, pages **59–71**, alongside the lesson-aligned glossary (pages **30–39**). Material here is original instruction and practice, not a textbook transcription.

## Learning objectives

- Ask whether a room is available and book a stay
- Ask about prices, breakfast and facilities
- Describe a room or apartment
- Express a simple problem with accommodation
- Give dates and larger numbers

## Follow this lesson in order

1. **[Grammar](grammar.md)** — clear rules, conjugations and examples.
2. **[Vocabulary](vocabulary.md)** — selected glossary words and useful chunks.
3. **[Exercises](exercise.md)** — self-tests, revealed solutions, speaking and a mini-mission.

## Original conversation sample

> Buonasera, avete una camera libera?  
> Sì, per quante notti?  
> Per due notti. La colazione è compresa?  
> Sì, è compresa nel prezzo.
