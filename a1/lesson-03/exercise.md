## Self-check: grammar and vocabulary

### 1. Complete: Io ___ un caffè. (prendere)

<details><summary>Show answer</summary>

**prendo**

Regular -ere: io → -o.

</details>

### 2. Complete: Noi ___ la pizza. (preferire)

<details><summary>Show answer</summary>

**preferiamo**

Preferire: noi → preferiamo.

</details>

### 3. Choose a polite order: Voglio / Vorrei un tè.

<details><summary>Show answer</summary>

**Vorrei un tè.**

Vorrei is more polite.

</details>

### 4. Pluralize: la pizza.

<details><summary>Show answer</summary>

**le pizze**

Feminine -a → -e.

</details>

### 5. Pluralize: il gelato.

<details><summary>Show answer</summary>

**i gelati**

Masculine -o → -i.

</details>

### 6. Choose: Questo panino è bene / buono.

<details><summary>Show answer</summary>

**buono**

An adjective describes the noun.

</details>

### 7. Translate: The bill, please.

<details><summary>Show answer</summary>

**Il conto, per favore.**

Useful restaurant chunk.

</details>

### 8. Translate: Two glasses of water, please.

<details><summary>Show answer</summary>

**Due bicchieri d'acqua, per favore.**

Plural bicchieri; d'acqua is common.

</details>
