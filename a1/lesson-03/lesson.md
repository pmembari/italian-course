## What you will learn

- Order a coffee, tea, juice or light snack in a **bar**.
- Order meals and drinks in a **trattoria/ristorante**; understand a menu.
- Make polite requests using **prendo**, **vorrei** and **per favore**.
- Talk about preferences with **preferire** and **volere**.
- Recognize items on a restaurant table and request missing objects.
- Ask for the bill, thank people, and respond politely.
- Read simple advertisements to choose a restaurant and plan a dinner menu.
- Book a table, say for how many people and specify a time.
- Use plural nouns, definite articles, **bene/buono**, and question words.
- Recall new vocabulary **from pictures, questions, sentences and spoken dialogues**.

## The five-line restaurant mission

Try this without looking at the dialogues:

1. **Greet:** `Buona sera, ...`
2. **Order:** `Vorrei ...`
3. **Choose:** `Preferisco ...`
4. **Request:** `Mi porta ..., per favore?`
5. **Pay and leave:** `Il conto, per cortesia. Grazie, arrivederci!`

Complete all five for a fictional meal, then repeat with different foods. Use the [shadowing and memory activities](vocabulary.md#shadowing-and-speaking-ladder) to practise rhythm and recall.
