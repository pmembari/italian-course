# Lezione 3 — Buon appetito!

[← A1 menu](../README.md) · **Overview** · [Grammar](grammar.md) · [Vocabulary](vocabulary.md) · [Exercise](exercise.md)

> **Order food and drinks politely and ask for the bill.** Follow *Nuovo Espresso 1*, pages **33–46**, alongside the lesson-aligned glossary (pages **17–25**). Material here is original instruction and practice, not a textbook transcription.

## Learning objectives

- Order drinks and food in a bar or restaurant
- Make polite requests and state preferences
- Identify basic food, drinks and table items
- Ask for missing items and request the bill

## Follow this lesson in order

1. **[Grammar](grammar.md)** — clear rules, conjugations and examples.
2. **[Vocabulary](vocabulary.md)** — selected glossary words and useful chunks.
3. **[Exercises](exercise.md)** — self-tests, revealed solutions, speaking and a mini-mission.

## Original conversation sample

> Buongiorno, che cosa prende?  
> Vorrei un panino e un'acqua minerale, per favore.  
> Desidera altro?  
> No, grazie. Il conto, per favore.

Try reading the dialogue, then change the fictional details and say it again.
