## Visual memory prompts — say a sentence, not a translation

| Picture | Chunk | Extend it into a question |
|---|---|---|
| 🚲 Bicycle | `Vado in bicicletta.` | `Vai spesso in bicicletta?` |
| 📖 Book | `Mi piace leggere.` | `Ti piace leggere?` |
| 🎶 Music | `Mi piace la musica.` | `Che musica ti piace?` |
| 🕒 Clock | `Sono le sette e mezza.` | `A che ora usciamo?` |
| 📅 Week | `Esco il sabato.` | `Esci ogni sabato?` |

**Mnemonic ≠ rule.** A picture suggests the meaning; the Italian article, verb form and preposition still need to be learned.

## English structure: a useful bridge and an important warning

- *I like dancing* → `Mi piace ballare`. Literal `Io piace ballare` is incorrect; the thing liked is the subject of `piacere`.
- *I like books* → `Mi piacciono i libri`. Plural **libri** requires `piacciono`.
- *I never ski* → `Non scio mai`. Italian typically combines **non + verb + mai**.
- *At seven* → `alle sette`. English `at` is not always translated by a single Italian preposition.

## Shadowing ladder (only with authorized Italian audio)

Strict **shadowing** requires an actual native-speaker recording. Without such audio, use the following as **read-aloud, echo repetition and memory recall**, not as verified pronunciation shadowing.

### Level 1 — repeat three times

`Mi piace ballare.` · `Non scio mai.` · `Esco spesso.` · `A che ora?` · `Alle otto.`

### Level 2 — original mini-dialogue

> **A:** Ti piace il cinema?
> **B:** Sì, ma preferisco leggere.
> **A:** Usciamo sabato sera?
> **B:** Volentieri! A che ora?
> **A:** Alle otto.

### Level 3 — without text

Replace at least **three details** (a day, activity and time; or room type, dates and facilities). Say the conversation with no reading, then check the grammar reference.

## Retrieval practice and spaced recall

1. Cover all translations; name five things in the pictures.
2. For each, make a statement and a question.
3. Return **tomorrow**, then after **three days**, then after **a week**. Begin with retrieval, **not** re-reading.
4. Interleave earlier lessons: introduce yourself (L1), talk about work/languages (L2), order food (L3), then use today's lesson.
