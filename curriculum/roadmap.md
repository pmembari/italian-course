# Italian study roadmap — generic reference

This is a flexible, non-personalized outline, not anyone's goal, schedule or certification plan.

| Stage | Common language topics | Example independent task |
|---|---|---|
| Beginner foundations | greetings, personal details, articles, subject pronouns, essere, avere, present tense | Introduce a fictional character |
| Everyday A1 | daily routines, shopping, directions, time, numbers, questions | Complete a simple shop dialogue |
| Intermediate beginner A2 | past experiences, comparisons, travel arrangements, invitations, simple messages | Write a short fictional travel message |
| Practice and consolidation | integrated reading, listening, speaking and writing | Complete original sample tasks and review corrections |

## General lesson method

- Retrieve previously studied words without cues.
- Work through a limited number of new examples.
- Transform examples into original sentences.
- Listen and shadow a short properly sourced Italian clip.
- Perform an original communication task.
- Review recurring grammar patterns.

Any individual learner's dates, proficiency, assessment results, preferences, and exam intentions belong in private notes, not here.
