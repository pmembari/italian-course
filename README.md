## Privacy

**This repository is public.** Keep it restricted to general learning material and anonymous examples. Do not publish personal study timelines, goals or exam plans, learner proficiency, assessment results, individually attributable errors, personal schedules, workplace details, personally identifying information, or private conversation content.

Personal learning records should stay in a separate **private** repository or be kept locally. Never copy them here without an explicit privacy review.

## Working with the repository

Record explanations in your own words and use short original examples. Reference textbook titles and page numbers only after inspecting the material. Do not upload copyrighted book pages, publisher recordings, or complete proprietary exams.
